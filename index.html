OPERATING SYSTEMS EXPERIMENT

The Problem: Alice has $1,000.00 in her account.

Suppose 50 concurrent threads attempt to withdraw $100 at the exact same millisecond.
• Expected Correct Behavior: Only 10 withdrawals should succeed ($1,000 ÷ $100 = 10). The other 40 must be rejected.
• Without Mutex: A Race Condition occurs in the Critical Section, allowing more than 10 people to withdraw money!
